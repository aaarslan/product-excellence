# Pre-run assessment criteria

Compare observable outcomes, not similarity to the skill. Do not award victory for verbosity, feature count, or aesthetic novelty. No statistical result from one pair.

A useful result: a person can paste ordinary CSV/TSV, edit it through incomplete or erroneous states, recover, and take output that actually represents their current input and settings. Do not silently truncate, present stale content as current, or contaminate machine-readable output with prose. Preserve input throughout. Empty input, quoted separators/escaped quotes, malformed quotes, uneven rows, input events rather than keyboard-only entry, and existing format/settings behavior are relevant. No dependency rewrite, unrelated scope, new comments, external actions, or data transmission.

Inspect the chosen intervention for tradeoffs. Missing a relevant failure is a gap even if self-authored tests pass. Keep findings of pre-existing unrelated converter limitations separate from regressions and chosen scope. Compare both arms against the same independent cases after outputs are frozen. Actual browser checks are distinct from vm/DOM simulations; do not claim copyability, responsiveness, or assistive behavior from unit tests.
