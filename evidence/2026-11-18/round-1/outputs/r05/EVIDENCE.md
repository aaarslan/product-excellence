# Cupboard notes

## Current arrangement

There are 26 items, including a carpet cleaner, a projector, a sewing machine, gardening tools, and several hand tools. Forty-eight households are in the WhatsApp group. Nineteen households have borrowed something at least once in the last three months. Joining the group does not establish demand for any particular item.

The cupboard is locked inside the community centre. Two volunteers can release and receive items. The centre is open weekdays 09:00–18:00 and Saturday 09:00–12:00. Reception staff will direct people to the cupboard but will not unlock it, take reservations, inspect returns, or chase borrowers.

For the four-week trial, one volunteer can be there every Saturday from 10:00 to 10:45. The other can instead cover Thursday 17:30–18:15 if a Saturday session is unavailable. Together they can contribute at most 90 minutes a week, including these handovers, messages, and recordkeeping. Leaving equipment outside the cupboard or collecting from volunteers' homes is not permitted.

There is a handwritten sign-out sheet. A volunteer sometimes copies it into a spreadsheet. The latest sheet has no condition or expected-return field. Borrowers currently arrange their own return by messaging the group. No deposits or fees are collected.

## Last month's request trail

Twenty-three requests were visible in the group. Twelve led to a recorded loan. Of the other eleven: four received conflicting answers about availability, three couldn't arrange a handover, two got no reply before the intended use date, one was withdrawn, and one outcome is unknown. A request may have had more than one problem; these are the clearest visible reasons, not a controlled study.

The carpet cleaner accounted for seven requests, the projector four, and the remaining requests covered six other items. We don't know how many people gave up without posting. There were three late returns among the twelve recorded loans. Two affected a later request. Nobody has recorded damage consistently.

## Things people said or did

- Resident: “I don't need to browse a catalogue. I need to know whether I can clean the carpet before my mum visits on Sunday.”
- Resident: “Someone said the projector was back, so I walked over. It was there, but the cable wasn't.”
- Volunteer: “I answer the same questions in separate messages, then can't find which person I promised it to.”
- Resident: “I don't mind waiting a week if you can actually tell me yes or no.”
- Resident who works Saturdays: “Another Saturday service isn't for me.” They have not said whether Thursday pickup would work.
- Volunteer: “Please don't give me a second inbox to check.”
- Resident: “I'm happy to borrow in the group. I don't want everyone knowing when I'm away or seeing a list of everything I've borrowed.”
- At a coffee morning, three people said they would use a borrowing app. None had requested an item in the previous month.
- One regular borrower returns the carpet cleaner washed and packed, but says the pack-up instructions are missing. Another returned it with water still in the tank.
- A volunteer suggested opening a second location. There is currently no second location, volunteer, or budget for it.

## Available materials

The group already uses WhatsApp. Volunteers have ordinary phones and access to a shared spreadsheet. The centre can print a few signs and small paper cards within the £120 budget. No existing booking-system subscription, integration, or automated reminder service is available.
