# A useful borrowing service?

Our community centre has a cupboard of things residents can borrow. People like the idea, but the current WhatsApp arrangement is unreliable. I've gathered the raw notes in `EVIDENCE.md`.

Choose one product or service concept worth trying with this group. It can include software, but the outcome matters more than building an app. Give me a concrete account of how a resident would use it, what we would have to operate, and why your choice fits what we actually know. If you think we should not run a pilot yet, make that decision and give the smallest useful next step.

We need a four-week trial we can arrange with six hours of preparation, at most £120 total, and at most 90 volunteer minutes per week including handovers. Only the access arrangements in the notes are available. We can't hire staff, add a smart lock, charge deposits, or require every resident to install an app. We also can't publish residents' names, contact details, or borrowing histories to the group.

Give me one recommendation, its limits, and what would make you continue, change, or stop after the trial. Maximum 600 words. Don't provide code, a slide deck, or a menu of unrelated ideas.
