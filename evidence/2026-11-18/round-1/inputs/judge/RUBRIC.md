# Frozen comparison criteria

Written from these fixtures before seeing any candidate skill or worker output. Keep this file out of worker context. Judge anonymized outputs without skill texts or arm identities.

## How to decide

For each acceptance condition, mark pass, partial, fail, or unverified. Then compare pairs: strongly prefer A / prefer A / tie / prefer B / strongly prefer B. Prefer ties unless a concrete difference changes success, comprehension, effort, recovery, or enjoyment. Do not total checked boxes or assign generic 10-point scores.

**Adequate** completes the requested job coherently within constraints. **Better** removes a demonstrated obstacle, makes an important action clearer, or creates a materially more useful complete experience. **Strongly better** changes practical success on a central outcome. A novel idea earns credit only through user benefit; jargon, confidence, length, feature count, and visual novelty alone do not. Judge implementation over promises.

## 1. Story Break

**Acceptance:** A single dependency-free HTML file works directly from disk and offline. A person can use three prompts, write, retain a draft, save, and reopen the complete story. Old draft and saved text remain intact, including old prompt strings absent from the current deck. The core activity works by keyboard and at narrow width.

Run the same probes on every artifact:

- Empty storage, laptop viewport: identify how to begin without the worker explanation; start a story. Does the experience give momentum or create setup and choice burden?
- With three prompts and a written sentence, seek a different place while retaining the character, complication, and writing. Equivalent mechanisms count equally.
- Finish four sentences, save, find the story again, and read it aloud. Is completion clear and reading comfortable? No particular layout or button is required.
- Write without a mandatory countdown. If a timer remains, check that expiry does not block or destroy work. Removing it is allowed.
- Attempt a new story or opening another while meaningful draft text exists. If deletion exists, try it. Assess actual protection/recovery and its burden; do not require a specific confirmation or undo pattern.
- Reload after writing and saving. Separately seed sample-storage.json and verify the dragon draft and both full saved stories. A safe schema migration is allowed.
- Exercise the core flow with keyboard only and at 390 CSS pixels wide; inspect focus, labels, readability, overflow, and reachable actions.

Weigh completion and preservation first, then beginning, creative agency, finishing, revisiting, and enjoyment across the stated ages. A polished form with unchanged friction is not automatically better; a restrained but thoughtfully invented activity can win. An attractive extra that gets in the way can lose. Report one best moment and one friction point per artifact. Do not call the judge walkthrough a child usability study.

## 2. Lending cupboard

**Acceptance:** At most 600 words; one recommendation or justified deferral. Fits six preparation hours, £120 total, available access, and 90 volunteer minutes weekly including handovers. No invented staff, locations, permissions, integrations, deposits, universal app installation, or public personal borrowing records. No fabricated demand or economics.

Compare whether the proposal:

- Finds a consequential user moment and addresses the underlying failure rather than reproducing unreliable promises in an app.
- Gives a credible path through availability, commitment, pickup, return, and readiness for the next borrower, including a clear no when necessary.
- Fits the volunteer workload without duplicate inboxes, constant manual updates, or unlimited chasing.
- Handles late returns, incomplete items, schedule exclusions, and unavailable volunteers honestly enough to keep its promise. Clear boundaries may beat features.
- Tests the important uncertainty with observable continue/change/stop evidence.

No hidden preferred concept: a narrower service, booking process, assisted request flow, service kit, or justified deferral can win. An inventive advantage must improve reliability, participation, or operating effort. Branding and expansion ambitions alone do not count.

## 3. Worktree command

**Acceptance:** At most 450 words; one bounded, implementable recommendation with command and key outcomes. Preserves old invocation semantics, --repo, forwarded arguments, child streams, and exit codes. No new default banner/prompt, forbidden Git mutation, dependencies, arbitrary directory discovery, config migration, or external integration. Failed new selection must never silently run in the current checkout.

Compare whether the proposal:

- Explains how intended checkout becomes known and accurately states the protection it provides.
- Reduces wrong-checkout execution with reasonable recurring effort and earns its place beside --repo.
- Safely handles missing/ambiguous/stale selections and detached HEAD without expanding scope unnecessarily.
- Makes execution root, config, forwarded arguments, failure status, and noninteractive behavior predictable.
- Names a few relevant compatibility/safety checks and a useful small trial.

Do not require a preferred flag or design. Context guards, explicit selection, or a justified smaller solution can all win. More abstraction is not an advantage by itself.

## 4. Literal scope case

For probe A, the complete response must be exactly: Your draft is saved on this device.

No preface, markup, quotation marks, or explanation. Pass/fail; report deviations separately. Optional probes B–D should satisfy their explicit narrow formats without product redesign.

## Report

For each case: acceptance outcomes, pairwise preference, concrete evidence for every non-tie, and untested uncertainties. Keep scope results separate. Do not invent an overall average. Three adequate ties do not demonstrate improvement; meaningful gains and regressions both matter.
