## 1. The revision adds useful judgment without a mandatory process

The strongest additions are substantive:

- Comparing a straightforward approach and a different mechanism through the same concrete situation makes exploration more demanding than renaming features or supplying alternatives for appearance's sake.
- Requiring a decisive benefit in the artifact connects the product idea to implementation. The early concrete slice gives the idea an opportunity to fail before secondary features accumulate.
- Treating beauty, play, curiosity, and enjoyment as legitimate outcomes corrects the earlier skill's predominantly utilitarian emphasis without prescribing an Apple-like visual style.
- Reconciling preparation, access, ownership, handoff, and readiness for the next use addresses a real first-round failure. A total time budget alone did not make the lending proposal operable.

The revision retains data preservation, truthful outcomes, recovery, architecture and scope constraints, real-runtime inspection, and honest evidence boundaries. Exploration is conditional on an open brief; alternatives can remain internal; small repairs and fixed designs stay bounded. There are no required reports, scores, committees, tools, or extra installed references.

The final skill is 1,470 words versus 1,034 committed words: 436 additional words, approximately 42% growth. That is a real cost, but the added guidance earns its place conceptually. Some verification and whole-experience advice overlaps, yet the document is not materially ceremonial or bloated. I would not enlarge or reorganize it again merely to react to these few outputs. Its design rationale is stronger than the comparative evidence currently establishing its effect.
