# A visitor page for Night Shift

I'm helping a small arts venue with **Night Shift**, an evening when visitors can explore the studios in our old paper mill. People usually arrive with someone else, have somewhere between 45 and 90 minutes, and aren't already art enthusiasts. At our trial, the printed programme sent people back to the front desk asking what they could fit in or whether there was anything their partner would enjoy.

Can you turn the attached event notes into a visitor experience we'd actually want to put in their hands? Give it a point of view. It should help someone arriving at the mill make a worthwhile visit, and feel like this particular place. We don't have a settled design or interaction in mind.

Make a functioning prototype as **one `index.html` file**, with everything embedded, that works when opened locally without an internet connection. No external packages, fonts, services, or assets. Most visitors will use a phone, though we need to review it on a laptop too. Assume it is **18:20 on the event evening** for the demo. Keep the build to something you can complete in about 15 minutes.

Use `CONTEXT.md` as the source of event facts. We cannot offer reservations or live availability. Deliver the file with a short note about what works and any material limitation.
