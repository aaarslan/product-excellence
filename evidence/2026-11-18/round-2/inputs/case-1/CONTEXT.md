# Night Shift: venue notes

These are fictional event materials for the prototype.

**Place:** Calder Mill, a converted paper mill beside the river. The evening is called **Night Shift** because the building once ran through the night. It is now home to small working studios. The people demonstrating are the makers themselves; the event should feel curious and welcoming, without pretending everyone knows art vocabulary.

**Hours:** 18:00–21:00. Admission and all six activities below are free. Visitors can leave whenever they like. This is a one-evening event; there is no ticket purchase or account.

| Activity | What happens | When / usual time spent | Setting and access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Printing Floor | Watch an old press turn a blank sheet into a two-colour river print. Ask the printer about the blocks on the wall. | Drop in, 18:00–21:00. About 20 minutes. | Ground floor. Step-free. A steady mechanical clatter; mostly standing, with a bench nearby. |
| Small Machines | A maker brings hand-built wooden automata to life: a swimming fish, a nodding bird, a very stubborn dog. | Demonstrations start at 18:30, 19:15, and 20:00. Each lasts 20 minutes. Best to join at the start. | Ground floor. Step-free. Some chairs. Small room; space is first come, first served. |
| River Listening Room | Sit with recordings from the river at dawn, under bridges, and during heavy rain. A short printed note explains each recording. | Drop in, 18:00–20:45. About 15–25 minutes. | Ground floor. Step-free. Quiet, dim, seated. |
| Make a Postcard | Use the studio's stamps and offcuts to make a postcard to take home. No drawing skill needed. | Drop in, 18:00–20:30. About 25 minutes once a seat is free. | Ground floor. Step-free. Seated. A wait is possible; we cannot estimate it live. |
| Repair Stories | Two furniture restorers show a battered chair and explain the choices behind its repair. Questions welcome. | Talks start at 18:45 and 20:00. Each lasts 30 minutes. | First floor, stairs only. Seated. Conversation rather than a hands-on workshop. |
| Archive Drawers | Open drawers of old mill labels, workers' notes, and print samples. Small details reward a close look. | Drop in, 18:00–21:00. About 10–15 minutes. | First floor, stairs only. Quiet and self-guided; mostly standing. |

The mill is compact: allow roughly three minutes between ground-floor rooms and five minutes when changing floors. These are planning estimates, not measured accessible travel times. There is no lift.

A ground-floor café has chairs and paid refreshments until 20:45. Toilets and the welcome desk are also on the ground floor. The café is a place to pause, not a seventh programmed activity.

The desk has paper programmes and can answer questions. Volunteers cannot update a website during the event. Room availability, postcard queues, and individual visitors' walking pace are unknown.
