# Choose a photo-scanning pilot

I manage a small public library. People keep asking for help turning family photographs into digital copies, and we have a scanner sitting unused. We've been given a six-week window and a small materials budget to try a service. I need to take **one concrete recommendation** to our planning meeting, rather than a menu of ideas.

The notes in `CONTEXT.md` are all we currently know. What should we actually offer during this window, to whom, and how should it work? I want a recommendation that makes good use of the opportunity without quietly handing an ongoing support job to the desk staff. Make clear what we would need to settle before the first participant arrives and what would tell us whether the trial deserved to continue.

Keep the entire answer to **650 words or fewer**. No code or external research is needed. You may narrow the offer, suggest a different way of delivering it, or recommend holding the public launch until a specific uncertainty is resolved. Don't contact anyone or make any real bookings or purchases.
