You’re welcome to wander. These are working studios, and the people showing you around are the makers themselves.

Four rooms are on the step-free ground floor. Repair Stories and Archive Drawers are upstairs, reached by stairs only.

The ground-floor café has chairs and paid refreshments until 20:45. Take a breather whenever you like; it’s separate from the six free activities.
