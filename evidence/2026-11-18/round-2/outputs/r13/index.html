    A place to pause

    The café is on the ground floor, with chairs and paid refreshments until 20:45. It’s a place to take a break between studios.

    Getting around

    Toilets and the welcome desk are on the ground floor. The first floor is reached by stairs only. There is no lift.

    A person to ask

    The welcome desk has paper programmes and can answer questions. The people demonstrating in the studios are the makers themselves. Questions are welcome.
