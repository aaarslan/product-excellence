# Night Shift — “A little of both”

**Output:** index.html, a single self-contained, dependency-free visitor prototype. Open it locally; it needs no connection.

The experience starts with a usable suggestion: Small Machines at 18:30, then the River Listening Room, with an estimated return to the welcome desk at 19:16. Visitors can choose 45, 60 or 90 minutes, pair two curiosities, and request a ground-floor route. Routes account for demonstration starts, activity lengths, closing times, transfers and the return walk. A visible compromise explains when an interest cannot fit.

Each stop includes an approachable introduction, access and seating information, and a conversational way in. Visitors can replace a stop using the time they are ready to move on, including after a wait. Earlier stops are retained; undo and restart are available. Postcard queue uncertainty is explicit. All six activities and venue essentials remain available below the route.

**Checked:** Inline JavaScript syntax and local jsdom initialization; all 216 duration/interest/access combinations; 216 first-stop swaps with undo; schedule, duration, closing-time, transfer and return-budget invariants; delayed and invalid recovery times; late return warnings; restart; programme selections; stairs and postcard caveats; six activities; unique IDs; no external resources.

**Limits:** Browser execution was unavailable. These are source/DOM/logic checks, not visual, layout, keyboard or real-user verification. Routes are suggestions, not reservations or availability checks. The demo begins at 18:20. Transfer estimates include walks from and back to the welcome desk; an additional five-minute planning allowance between upstairs rooms is disclosed.
