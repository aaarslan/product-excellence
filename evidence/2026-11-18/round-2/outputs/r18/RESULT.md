# Run “Five favourites”: a booked, assisted scanning trial

Offer a free, 60-minute appointment to copy **five personally important prints**, with a volunteer alongside the participant. The promised finish is five digital photographs opened on the participant’s or accompanying helper’s own computer, a take-home USB drive, and a short guide to finding the files again.

Target residents who can attend Wednesday mornings, choose five ordinary loose prints, and bring a computer that can read the agreed USB drive. Someone with boxes can participate by selecting five beforehand. Explicitly exclude drop-off collections, slides, phone transfers and general device support. Requiring a compatible computer excludes some residents; record those exclusions rather than pretending this meets everyone’s needs.

This offers companionship and a useful keepsake without assuming self-service works. The volunteer operated nearly everything in both trials; one participant left with nothing because transfer failed. Enquiries suggest interest, but overlapping counts and unknown availability do not establish demand for this particular offer.

## Schedule and ownership

Use **week one for preparation and rehearsal**. Offer two appointments in each of weeks two, three, five and six: **10:10–11:10 and 11:20–12:20**, eight places total. The remaining 30 minutes cover opening, turnover and closing. Advertise no session in week four. Release later places after reviewing the first pair.

The volunteer owns all scanning, troubleshooting, file clearance and room reset within their existing Wednesday window. If they are unavailable, cancel; desk staff do not substitute. Do not count the uncommitted second volunteer.

Cap paid staff work at 90 minutes weekly: 30 for bookings and reminders, 30 for preparation/closing checks, and 30 for review and scheduled follow-up. In week one, use that allowance for setup and recruitment instead. Log actual minutes; if work exceeds the allowance, reduce appointments or pause. Ordinary bookings are the desk’s only participant-facing responsibility.

## Settle before inviting participants

The manager must approve the scope, responsibility split, booking wording and cancellation process. Booking information must state that technical help ends with the appointment. Price eight take-home USB drives, two spares and printed guides within £300; reserve compatible drives against bookings and avoid unnecessary expenditure.

The volunteer must rehearse the whole session: suitable print handling, a consistent JPEG scan setting, readable filenames, USB export, opening exported files on a second computer, and clearing the guest account. Confirm a working spare-drive recovery route. Hold public appointments until this complete path works within the time budget.

Invite interest through the existing enquiries and history group, using one deduplicated booking list. Confirm the actual Wednesday date, five-print limit, computer/connector compatibility and intended use of the copies before allocating a place. A request for an evening or phone service is unmet demand, not an eligible booking.

## What happens in the room

Start with the favourite photograph. Scan it, export it immediately, and have the participant open it on their own computer. This makes the first satisfying moment a usable photograph and exposes transfer problems before more work accumulates.

Then complete the other four. Invite the participant to operate one scan with coaching; observe whether they can repeat the steps. Reserve the final 15 minutes for opening all five exported files, optionally copying them onto their computer, returning originals and explaining the guide. Finish without retaining photographs or passwords.

If export fails, try the rehearsed spare route within the appointment. If unresolved, record a failed handoff, agree deletion, and pause further appointments until repaired. Never redirect technical problems to the desk.

## Decide whether to continue

Review bookings, attendance, exclusions, usable handoffs, observed independence, actual time and costs. Ask once, within the staff allowance, whether participants reopened or shared their copies. Continue only with demonstrated demand for this exact offer, dependable usable handoffs, bounded support and confirmed future resources. Independence informs any later self-service proposal; it is not yet established.
