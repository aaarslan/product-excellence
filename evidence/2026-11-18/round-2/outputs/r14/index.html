03 / A place with a past

The mill used to
work through the night.

Tonight, follow the people making things inside it.

Calder Mill was a paper mill beside the river. Its studios are now small working spaces, and the people showing you around their work are the makers themselves.

A pause, if you need one

The ground-floor café has chairs and paid refreshments until 20:45. All six programmed activities are free.
