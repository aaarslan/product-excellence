# Recommendation: a small, assisted photo-to-USB clinic

Offer free, booked 55-minute appointments for residents to copy **up to ten preselected prints**, prioritising five favourites. Success means leaving with usable files and knowing how to find them. The volunteer provides assistance; independent scanning is a learning opportunity, not an advertised prerequisite or promised outcome.

Target residents who can attend Wednesday mornings and bring a laptop that reads USB, or attend with a trusted helper who brings one. People with large collections may select ten prints; do not accept boxes for drop-off. Exclude slides, phone extraction, restoration and untested cloud transfers. Requiring a usable receiving device narrows access, but addresses the failed handoff seen in the second trial.

## Schedule and commitment

Use week one for preparation and an end-to-end rehearsal. Offer two appointments on each Wednesday in weeks two, three, five and six: **eight appointments maximum**. Week four is closed. Run appointments at 10:10–11:05 and 11:15–12:10, leaving ten minutes for setup, ten between participants and twenty for closing within the volunteer’s 10:00–12:30 availability.

This is capacity, not a demand forecast. The 24 enquiries, approximately 19 people and history group’s eight interested members may overlap; none represents an accepted appointment. Invite existing enquirers and the group to the specific offer, deduplicate bookings, and record device and timing exclusions. If suitable residents do not accept appointments, hold the public launch rather than widening into unsupported services.

## Settle before inviting participants

The manager and existing volunteer must rehearse the complete journey using sample prints: scan, check legibility and orientation, save ordinary JPEGs, export, disconnect, reopen from USB on a receiving laptop, and clear the guest session. Test a spare drive and recovery after an interrupted session. Do not launch until this works reliably.

Confirm take-home drives, a spare and a short illustrated guide fit within £300; check connector compatibility when booking. Confirm the volunteer’s five dates and who cancels appointments if they are absent. The uncommitted second volunteer adds no capacity.

Reserve the paid staff allowance explicitly: 30 minutes for bookings/reminders, 20 for preparation/closing, 20 for feedback/records and 20 contingency weekly. In week one, use that allowance for preparation and rehearsal instead. The volunteer owns technical work. Publish the pilot’s end date and make clear that desk staff cannot troubleshoot devices or provide continuing scanning support.

## Participant journey and recovery

At booking, explain the limits, confirm the receiving laptop and intended use, and ask participants to choose and order their prints beforehand. Keep originals with their owner throughout.

Start by scanning one priority print and testing its export on the participant’s laptop. This exposes transfer problems before most of the appointment is spent scanning. If it works, continue, stopping scanning with fifteen minutes remaining for handoff. Have the participant open files from their drive, check their priority pictures and locate the clearly named folder themselves. Offer one supervised scan if time permits; observe assistance needed.

If transfer fails, try the tested spare. If still unsuccessful, stop; return originals and offer only an available pilot appointment after resolving the cause. Explain deletion before starting: nothing remains after logout, and no passwords are collected. Never record an unsuccessful transfer as completion.

## Decide whether to continue

Record accepted bookings, attendance, completed handoffs, help needed, actual staff/volunteer minutes, materials spending and desk interruptions. One brief, scheduled follow-up within the pilot asks whether participants opened and used their copies; it offers no troubleshooting commitment.

Agree at the planning meeting what demonstrated use and further eligible demand would justify reserving these hours again. At week six, assess that evidence alongside workload. Continue only with a confirmed room, volunteer and materials allocation, dependable handoffs, and paid work within 90 minutes weekly. Unresolved transfer failures or desk spillover require redesign before extension. Assess independent use separately; these trials cannot yet justify self-service.
