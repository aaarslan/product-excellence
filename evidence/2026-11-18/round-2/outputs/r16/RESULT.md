# Recommendation: a booked “Ten Treasured Photos” clinic

Run a free, volunteer-led, one-to-one print-scanning pilot. Use week one to rehearse the complete process and confirm bookings, then offer **eight 60-minute appointments**: 10:10–11:10 and 11:20–12:20 on Wednesdays in weeks two, three, five and six. The volunteer’s remaining 30 minutes cover setup, turnover and closing. Advertise no session in week four.

This fits the available staffing and the request for someone alongside a beginner. The trials show that scanning alone is insufficient: one person received 18 copies, while another received none after transfer failed. They do not establish that residents can operate independently.

## Who and what

Offer appointments to residents with **up to ten preselected, ordinary loose prints** and a confirmed way to open USB files afterwards. Box owners are welcome if they select ten beforehand. Each person keeps their originals throughout. Promise help making a small, useful collection within an hour; ten is a ceiling, not a guaranteed output.

Provide a tested USB drive if needed. At booking, establish where the person will actually view or share the files. A phone-only participant needs a separately verified compatible route before being accepted; this pilot will not troubleshoot phones. Exclude slides, restoration, whole-box processing and unattended drop-offs. Make clear that the trial ends after six weeks and includes no continuing technical support.

Invite previous enquirers and the history group through the same booking process. Deduplicate names, confirm Wednesday attendance and record collection type and intended destination. Neither enquiry totals nor the group’s eight interested members constitute bookings. Keep a short waiting list and separately count requests excluded by timing or format.

## Settle before public appointments

The manager should sign off on:

- **Delivery:** the volunteer’s dates, a cancellation procedure and who owns the appointment list. If the volunteer is unavailable, cancel; desk staff do not substitute.
- **A complete rehearsal:** scan representative prints, name files, export to the intended drive type, safely reconnect it and open the copies. Confirm compatibility with participants’ stated destination before accepting bookings. Test the guest-account cleanup and write a short operating checklist. Hold appointments until this works reliably.
- **Privacy and handling:** explain temporary storage, obtain agreement to deletion after verified handoff, and establish what happens if export fails. Never request passwords. Return originals and clear temporary files before the next participant; a failed handoff counts as a failure and pauses further sessions until resolved.
- **Materials and workload:** approve drive quantities and costs, sleeves and printed instructions within £300. Set a weekly staff allowance of 45 minutes for bookings, 30 for preparation/review and 15 for contingencies; week-one rehearsal must fit that same 90-minute total. Reduce bookings if administration exceeds it.

## The appointment

Spend roughly ten minutes agreeing the participant’s goal and practising one scan, thirty minutes scanning together, and the final twenty exporting and checking. Stop scanning early if necessary to protect handoff time. Let the participant attempt a scan and save operation, with help available; record the assistance needed.

Reopen the exported files, check image quality together and ask the participant to demonstrate or explain how they will access them afterwards. Give a one-page guide covering filenames, opening the drive and making another copy. Avoid promising untested home scanner compatibility.

## Decide whether to repeat

Record attendance, successful take-home collections, goal achieved, assistance needed, technical failures and actual staff minutes. Use an optional single follow-up question to check whether copies opened at the intended destination, within the administration allowance.

Recommend another bounded round if at least six participants achieve usable take-home collections, transfer problems are resolved, staff stays within 90 minutes weekly, and room and volunteer commitments are renewed. This is a practical local threshold, not proof of wider demand. Learning observations should guide future instruction; they do not yet justify self-service. Low uptake should prompt scrutiny of Wednesday access and eligibility before concluding that residents lack interest.
